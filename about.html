About the Organization

Built by students,
for students who build.

iConnect is a technology-focused school organization that promotes innovation, creativity, collaboration, and technical excellence among students.

Club History

iConnect began as a small group of students who shared a curiosity for code and design, meeting informally to trade tutorials and build side projects. That informal circle grew into a recognized school organization, formalizing committees around the technology fields its founding members were already passionate about — and it has expanded every year since through workshops, competitions, and community projects.

Mission

To equip students with practical technology skills and a collaborative mindset — giving every member a space to learn, build, and lead real projects alongside peers who share their curiosity.

Vision

A student community recognized for producing confident, technically skilled, and socially responsible innovators who carry what they learn in iConnect into college, careers, and their communities.

Objectives

Run regular workshops across every technology field the club covers, give members hands-on project and competition experience, document club activities transparently, and maintain a member-run digital platform that reflects the club's own standards of quality.

What We Stand For

Core Values

Innovation

Encouraging original thinking and new approaches to problems.

Collaboration

Building together across skill levels and committees.

Excellence

Holding our work — and each other — to a high standard.

Integrity

Acting honestly in how we build, credit, and share work.

Membership

Benefits of joining iConnect

  • Hands-on workshops led by peers and the club adviser across every technology field.

  • Real project and portfolio experience, from websites to robotics builds.

  • A community of students who share your curiosity and push each other forward.

  • Opportunities to compete, present, and lead committees as an officer.
